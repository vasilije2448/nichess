Attacking

The number next to each piece shows its health points.

When you perform a standard attack, one of two things happens:

1. If your piece has enough ability points to destroy the enemy piece, it moves to its position.

2. Otherwise, it moves toward the enemy piece and stops one square away. The attacked piece then loses health points equal to the attacker's ability points.

King

Health points: 10

Ability points: 60

The king moves one square in any direction: horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. The game is over when the king loses its health points.

Castling

Castling is a special move that allows the king to move two squares horizontally towards the pirate and then moving the pirate to the square that the king passed over. The king and the pirate must be in their original positions, and there must be no pieces between them.

Note: Unlike in chess, it does not matter whether the square the king passes over is attacked by an enemy piece.

Note: Unlike in chess, it does not matter whether the pieces have moved before.

Queen

Health points: 10

Ability points: 30

The queen moves any number of squares in any direction: horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. Queens have the most versatile movement of all the pieces.

Queen-Maenad ability

When placed 1 square diagonally to an allied maenad, the queen can throw the maenad and destroy the first enemy piece in its path. Neighbouring enemy pieces will take 20 damage.

Pawn

Health points: 30

Ability points: 60

The pawn moves forward one square, except when attacking a piece diagonally. It can move two squares forward from its starting position. Pawns are the only pieces that attack differently from how they move.

Note: Unlike in chess, there is no en passant rule in this game.

Pawn promotion

When a pawn reaches the last row, it transforms into a pirate.

Maenad

Health points: 10

Ability points: 30

The maenad moves diagonally any number of squares and also one square in any other direction.

Pirate

Health points: 60

Ability points: 30

The pirate moves horizontally or vertically any number of squares.

Pirate-Pirate ability

When placed 1 square horizontally or vertically to an allied pirate, the pirate can throw the allied pirate and destroy the first enemy piece in its path. Neighbouring enemy pieces will take 20 damage.

Knight

Health points: 60

Ability points: 60

The knight moves in an L-shape: two squares in one direction and then one square perpendicular to that direction. Knights are the only pieces that can jump over other pieces.

Threefold Repetition

If the same position occurs three times, the game automatically ends in a draw.

333-Move Rule

If the game reaches move 333, it automatically ends in a draw.

Note: Move here refers to an action taken by a single player. Two Nichess moves are equivalent to one chess move.
